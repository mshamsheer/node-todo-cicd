If you're interviewing for a ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus (On-Premises) Administrator, Support Engineer, or ITSM Analyst role, here are some commonly asked interview questions with sample answers.

1. What is ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus?

Answer: ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus is an IT Service Management (ITSM) and Help Desk solution used to manage incidents, service requests, assets, changes, problems, and IT operations. It follows ITIL best practices and is available in both Cloud and On-Premises deployments. It provides ticket management, asset management, CMDB, self-service portal, SLA management, and workflow automation capabilities.

2. What is the difference between ServiceDesk Plus On-Premises and Cloud?

Answer: In the On-Premises version, the application is installed and maintained on the organization's servers, giving full control over data, customization, and security. In the Cloud version, ManageEngine hosts and manages the application, reducing infrastructure and maintenance responsibilities.

3. What are the main modules in ServiceDesk Plus?

Answer: The major modules include:

Incident Management
Service Request Management
Asset Management
Problem Management
Change Management
CMDB
Project Management
Purchase Management
Knowledge Base
Reports and Dashboards
4. What is Incident Management?

Answer: Incident Management is the process of restoring normal service operation as quickly as possible after an interruption. In ServiceDesk Plus, incidents are logged as tickets and assigned to technicians for resolution.

Example: A user cannot access Outlook. A ticket is created and assigned to the Messaging Team for resolution.

5. What is a Service Request?

Answer: A service request is a request from a user for information, access, or a standard service.

Examples:

New laptop request
VPN access request
Software installation request

Unlike incidents, service requests are generally planned and pre-approved activities.

6. What is an SLA?

Answer: SLA stands for Service Level Agreement.

It defines:

Response time
Resolution time
Escalation rules

Example: High Priority Ticket:

First Response: 15 minutes
Resolution: 4 hours

If SLA is breached, automatic notifications and escalations can be triggered.

7. What are Business Rules in ServiceDesk Plus?

Answer: Business Rules are automation rules used to perform actions on tickets automatically.

Examples:

Auto-assign tickets based on category.
Change priority depending on keywords.
Route hardware requests to Infrastructure Team.
Send notifications automatically.
8. What is CMDB?

Answer: CMDB (Configuration Management Database) stores information about Configuration Items (CIs) and their relationships.

Examples of CIs:

Servers
Routers
Applications
Databases
Virtual Machines

CMDB helps identify the impact of changes and incidents across IT infrastructure.

9. What is Change Management?

Answer: Change Management controls modifications to IT systems to minimize business risk.

Change lifecycle:

Create Change Request
Risk Assessment
Approval Process
Implementation
Review and Closure

Example: Upgrading a production server requires approval before implementation.

10. What is Problem Management?

Answer: Problem Management identifies the root cause of recurring incidents.

Example: If users repeatedly lose network connectivity, the underlying switch issue is investigated and permanently fixed.

Incident = Fix quickly. Problem = Find root cause.

11. What is Asset Management?

Answer: Asset Management tracks IT assets throughout their lifecycle.

Assets include:

Laptops
Desktops
Servers
Printers
Software licenses

Benefits:

Inventory control
License compliance
Warranty tracking
Audit readiness
12. How do emails become tickets in ServiceDesk Plus?

Answer: ServiceDesk Plus can automatically convert incoming emails into tickets.

Process:

User sends email to support mailbox.
ServiceDesk Plus fetches the email.
A ticket is automatically created.
Technician receives the request.
13. What is a Self-Service Portal?

Answer: The Self-Service Portal allows users to:

Create tickets
Check ticket status
Access knowledge articles
View announcements
Submit service requests

This reduces help desk call volume.

Answer: Priority is typically determined by:

Impact
Single User
Department
Organization
Urgency
Low
Medium
High

Example:

15. How do you assign tickets automatically?

Answer: Using:

Business Rules
Technician Auto Assignment
Round Robin Assignment
Category-Based Assignment

Example: Network-related tickets are automatically assigned to the Network Team.

16. What are the different statuses of a ticket?

Answer: Common statuses:

Open
Assigned
In Progress
Pending User Response
Resolved
Closed

Organizations may customize these statuses.

17. How would you handle a high-priority ticket?

Answer:

Acknowledge immediately.
Verify impact.
Assign to appropriate support group.
Notify stakeholders.
Escalate if required.
Resolve within SLA.
Document resolution.
18. What reporting features are available?

Answer: ServiceDesk Plus provides reports for:

Ticket volume
SLA compliance
Technician performance
Resolution time
Customer satisfaction
Asset inventory
Change success rate

Managers use these reports for operational reviews.

19. What are the advantages of On-Premises deployment?

Answer:

Complete data ownership
Greater customization
Internal security controls
Easier compliance with regulations
Database access and integration flexibility
20. Why should we hire you for a ServiceDesk Plus Administrator role?

Sample Answer:

"I have strong knowledge of ITSM processes including Incident, Problem, Change, and Asset Management. I understand ServiceDesk Plus administration, SLA configuration, ticket lifecycle management, workflow automation, and reporting. I can support end users, optimize service desk operations, and ensure compliance with ITIL best practices. My goal is to improve service quality while reducing ticket resolution times."

Scenario-Based Question
Interviewer:

"Users report that they cannot access VPN. What would you do?"

Answer:
Create/verify incident ticket.
Check if multiple users are affected.
Review VPN server health.
Check authentication logs.
Verify network connectivity.
Escalate to Network Team if necessary.
Communicate updates to users.
Resolve and document root cause.
Close ticket after user confirmation.

These 20 questions cover about 80-90% of ServiceDesk Plus Administrator/Support Engineer interview rounds.
